## About the programme

ESTALMAT is an initiative of the Spanish Royal Academy of Exact, Physical and
Natural Sciences, dedicated to identifying and nurturing early mathematical
talent among secondary school students. Entry is by competitive examination, and
sessions are held at the university outside school hours across several academic
years.

## What we actually did

It was not accelerated school mathematics: it was problems in logic,
combinatorics, geometry and number theory, deliberately posed so that no
memorised method would apply. The point was to attack a problem without knowing
in advance where to start.

## Why I include it

I attended for three academic years, between the ages of 13 and 16, and it is
probably the origin of how I approach technical problems. I learned early that
being stuck for a long stretch is a normal part of the process, not a sign that
something is going wrong.
