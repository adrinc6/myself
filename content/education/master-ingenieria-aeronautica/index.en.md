## About the programme

UPM's Master's in Aeronautical Engineering is the degree that qualifies you to
practise as a professional Aeronautical Engineer in Spain. It goes deeper into
the design and analysis of complete aerospace systems, beyond the fundamentals
covered by the undergraduate degree.

## Specialty

My specialty is **Aerospace Systems and Air Transport**, focused on
aircraft-level systems integration and on air transport as a complex operational
system.

## Studying alongside full-time work

I am taking the master's while working full time as an AI & Data Engineer. The
two feed each other: the analytical rigour of aeronautical engineering helps me
frame data problems properly, and working daily with Python and models gives me
tools for the numerical analysis the master's demands.
