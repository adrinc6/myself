## About the programme

UPM's Aerospace Engineering degree covers the fundamentals of the discipline:
aerodynamics, fluid mechanics, structural mechanics, propulsion, orbital
mechanics and control systems, with a heavy load of applied mathematics and
physics.

## Specialty

I took the **Aerospace Sciences and Technologies** track, the branch most
oriented towards scientific fundamentals and the technical analysis of the
aerospace vehicle.

## What I took away

Beyond the specific content, the degree left me with a way of working: break a
large problem into tractable parts, model before calculating, and distrust a
result until you have cross-checked it. It is the same way of thinking I now
apply to data work and language models.

It is also where I learned to program with a purpose: MATLAB and Python as tools
for solving engineering problems, rather than as ends in themselves.
