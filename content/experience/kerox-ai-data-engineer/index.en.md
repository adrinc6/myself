## The role

I design and implement AI-powered backend solutions to process and analyse text
and audio data. The work spans everything from data ingestion and processing
through to putting the resulting tools into production.

## What I focus on

**Large language model integration.** Bringing LLMs into real products, with
everything that entails: prompt design, handling model responses, controlling
edge cases, and deciding where a model genuinely adds value over a simpler
deterministic solution.

**Metric-driven model improvement.** Measure before changing. Define what
"better" means for each specific case, build the evaluation around it, and use
those numbers to decide the next steps rather than going on impressions.

**AI tooling.** Building the internal pieces that let the rest of the team move
faster.

## How I work

I drive projects proactively, proposing next steps rather than waiting to be
assigned them, in constant coordination with the rest of the team. A good part
of the value lies in spotting early what is worth building and what is not.
