## The project

I developed a data-matching tool in Python and PostgreSQL, designed to compare
and rank large datasets efficiently.

## The scoring algorithm

The core piece was designing a **custom scoring algorithm** to compare and rank
records. The underlying problem in data matching is that exact equality is
almost never enough: real-world data arrives with typos, inconsistent formats
and incomplete fields, so you need a measure of how similar two records are and
a criterion for deciding at what point they count as the same one.

Working over large datasets added the constraint that the comparison had to be
efficient, not merely correct.
