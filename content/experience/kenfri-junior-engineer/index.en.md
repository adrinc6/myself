## The role

A summer position at an engineering firm, supporting the early stages of several
mechanical installation projects.

## Responsibilities

- **Drafting plans in AutoCAD** for the projected installations.
- **Fluid dynamics calculations** applied to sizing the installations.
- **Preparing budgets and material orders** using Excel.

## What I learned

This was my first contact with engineering outside the classroom, and it mostly
showed me the distance between a well-executed calculation and a project that
can actually be built: deadlines, available materials and budget shape the
technical solution just as much as the numbers do.
